Student excellence

Achievements

Awards, recognitions, and learning milestones that reflect technical skill, creativity, teamwork, and growth.

Recognition Milestones
Competition recognition

Ideas transformed into impact

Champion · 2025

Project XP wins championship

Project XP: Experience Digital Growth in Laguna’s Arcade of Learning was named Champion in the Kaunlarang Teknolohikal category of Sibol ng Laguna 2025.

The achievement recognizes the team’s creativity, innovation, and commitment to using technology to support digital learning and community growth.

Third place

Team Cabuyao reaches the podium

Team Cabuyao earned Third Place in the Byte Forward Hackathon Grand Finals.

The team included BS Computer Science students William Jefferson J. Demillo, Mark Genessy I. Mamore, and Vince Harold C. Sualog, together with BS Electronics Engineering student Aaron Charles Abenes. Their accomplishment reflects teamwork, technical skill, creativity, and problem-solving ability.

IMPLEMENTATION · JUNE 23, 2026

Project XP reaches San Isidro Elementary School

Project XP: Experience Digital Growth in Laguna’s Arcade of Learning continued its implementation at San Isidro Elementary School on June 23, 2026, reaching another group of young learners through interactive games and engaging activities.

The session introduced pupils to essential digital literacy topics, including online safety, identifying misinformation, and responsible use of technology, while encouraging learning through curiosity, teamwork, and participation.

With three schools reached, Project XP continues its mission of making digital literacy more accessible, engaging, and meaningful for young learners.

SEMIFINALIST · 2025

CCS delegates advance to CodeChum National Programming Challenge Semifinals

Ten CCS delegates successfully advanced to the Semifinals of the CodeChum National Programming Challenge 2025, showcasing their programming skills, problem-solving abilities, teamwork, and determination.

Guided by Asst. Prof. Janus Raymond C. Tan, the delegates represented the CCS community as they competed against fellow student programmers in the national challenge.

Their advancement marks another achievement for the CCS community as they prepare to continue their journey in the semifinal round on November 25.

TOP 10 OVERALL · 2025

Sigurado project earns Top 10 Overall recognition

“Sigurado: Sistemang Gabay sa Ulat at Responde Agad – Digital at Online,” a project by the Association of Computer Science Students (ACSS), earned a spot among the Top 10 Overall Projects of Sibol ng Laguna 2025.

The project was developed by Andrew Dejito, Micah Chelsea Delacruz, Mark Francis Entredicho, Emmanuel Victorio, and Joshua Emmanuel Balois, showcasing the team’s innovation, collaboration, and commitment to creating technology-driven solutions.

This recognition marks another significant achievement for the ACSS community, reflecting its dedication to innovation, excellence, and using technology to serve the community.

2ND PLACE · 2025

Christian V. Balinado Secures 2nd Place in InterCICSkwela Programming Competition

Christian V. Balinado, a second-year BSCS student and ACSS Public Relations Officer, proudly secured 2nd Place in the InterCICSkwela Online Competitive Programming Competition (Collegiate Level).

The competition was hosted by the BSU-TNEU College of Informatics and Computing Sciences at the Alangilan Campus on April 26, 2025. Christian demonstrated outstanding programming skills, logical thinking, and determination throughout the competition.

His achievement brings pride and honor to the entire ACSS community and inspires fellow Computer Science students to continue developing their skills and striving for excellence.
