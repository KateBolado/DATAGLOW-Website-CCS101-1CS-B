How we work

Committees

Dedicated teams translate ACSS plans into organized programs, clear communication, reliable records, and well-supported activities.

Working together

Seven areas of support

Each committee contributes a distinct set of skills while collaborating toward the organization’s shared goals.

01

Committee on Operations and Logistics

Responsible for the planning and logistical needs of ACSS activities. The committee helps ensure that events are organized, prepared, and run smoothly from setup to completion.

  • Coordinates venues, equipment, and materials
  • Assists with event setup and logistics
  • Supports activities before, during, and after programs
02

Committee on Student Relations and Representation

Serves as a bridge between ACSS and the student community. The committee promotes student involvement, listens to concerns, and helps create a welcoming and connected organization.

  • Promotes student participation and engagement
  • Helps communicate student concerns and feedback
  • Strengthens connections within the CS community
03

Committee on Student Development

Focuses on activities that support the personal, academic, and professional growth of Computer Science students through meaningful learning opportunities.

  • Supports workshops, seminars, and training
  • Encourages leadership and skill development
  • Helps organize student-centered programs
04

Committee on Partnerships and Career Linkages

Builds connections between ACSS and external organizations, professionals, and potential partners to create opportunities that benefit Computer Science students.

  • Coordinates partnerships and collaborations
  • Supports career-related programs and opportunities
  • Communicates with external organizations and partners
05

Committee on Financial Management

Supports the proper management of funds for ACSS activities and helps maintain organized and transparent financial records.

  • Assists in preparing budgets for activities
  • Records and monitors financial transactions
  • Helps maintain accurate financial documentation
06

Committee on Audit and Compliance

Helps ensure that ACSS activities, records, and financial transactions follow proper procedures and organizational guidelines.

  • Reviews financial and activity records
  • Checks documents for accuracy and completeness
  • Promotes accountability and transparency
07

Committee on Media and Communications

Manages the organization's communication and creative content to keep students informed and strengthen the public presence of ACSS.

  • Creates publicity materials and digital content
  • Documents and promotes ACSS activities
  • Manages announcements and social media content
One organization

Different roles, shared direction

Committee work connects technical knowledge with communication, documentation, and operations. Together, these teams help ACSS deliver organized and meaningful experiences for Computer Science students.

Interested in contributing?

Membership is open to enrolled Computer Science students. Contact ACSS to ask about current participation opportunities.
