Program areas

Experiences designed for student growth

ACSS activities combine technical development with collaboration, leadership, and meaningful participation.

01

Programming workshops

Guided sessions help students strengthen coding fundamentals, explore concepts, and gain confidence through practice.

02

Technical sessions

Skill-building activities introduce tools, approaches, and computing topics that complement academic study.

03

Competitions

Programming and technology challenges encourage creative problem-solving, teamwork, and university representation.

04

Student forums

Open discussions create space for ideas, concerns, shared learning, and stronger connections in the CS community.

Explore this month's academic programs, student support initiatives, collaborative activities, and opportunities for ACSS members.

Event date September42026
COLLABORATIVE LEARNING

Study Sync: Collaborative Reviewer Hub

A collaborative academic resource hub where student-made reviewers are collected, screened, organized, and shared to help BSCS students prepare for major examinations.

Venue
Online / Digital Repository
Event date September92026
CREATIVE ACCESS

GraphiX Grant: Creative Access Program

A creative support program that provides ACSS members access to available premium design tools to strengthen their creativity, digital communication, and academic presentation skills.

Venue
Online / Digital Program
Event date September152026
EXAM READINESS

E-SALANG: ACSS Exam Readiness Initiative

Practice examinations designed to help BSCS students prepare for prelim, midterm, and final examinations while identifying topics that may require further review.

Venue
Google Forms / Official E-Salang Platform
Event date September212026
PEER LEARNING

PeerLab: Peer Learning and Academic Enrichment Series

Structured peer-learning sessions where Computer Science students can review lessons, practice exercises, share knowledge, and receive academic support from qualified student volunteers.

Venue
CCS Classrooms / Google Meet
Event date September262026
STUDENT RESOURCE SUPPORT

CalcUShare: Lend-A-Calculator Initiative

A calculator lending initiative that gives Computer Science students temporary access to scientific calculators while encouraging responsible borrowing and resource-sharing.

Venue
College of Computing Studies Office

NEXUS Innovation Circle: Connecting Partners. Creating Possibilities.

Venue
Online Platforms / Approved Campus or Partner Venues
