Background

How ACSS Began

The Association of Computer Science Students traces its roots to the development of the BS Computer Science program at the University of Cabuyao.

After a period of inactivity, the organization was officially re-established on October 23, 2020—a significant date proudly reflected on its official seal.

Its revival was supported by dedicated educators and student leaders who wanted to provide Computer Science students with a united academic community and a support system for their academic and professional goals.

Re-established

October 23, 2020

The date represents a renewed commitment to learning, cooperation, leadership, and student development.

Purpose and role

A Home and Voice for CS students

ACSS serves as the academic home and collective voice of Computer Science students at the University of Cabuyao.

It supports students through programming workshops, technical sessions, competitions, student forums, and other activities that develop academic and professional abilities.

The organization also strengthens connections among students, educators, university organizations, and industry professionals while encouraging innovation, teamwork, responsible leadership, and the meaningful use of technology.

Milestones

The ACSS Journey

  1. October 23, 2020

    Official re-establishment

    ACSS was officially re-established as the academic organization of BS Computer Science students at the University of Cabuyao.

  2. Early development

    A stronger identity and direction

    Dr. Renelina D. Mañabo, Mr. Neil Tristan M. Alcabasa, and Asst. Prof. Evangelina A. Magaling helped establish an organized structure and clear direction for the revived organization.

  3. Growth and activities

    Learning beyond the classroom

    ACSS began conducting programming workshops, technical skill-building sessions, student forums, and academic activities while strengthening collaboration across the university community.

  4. 2025

    Notable recognition

    Under the leadership of Christian Balinado, ACSS won the Championship in the Kaunlarang Teknolohikal category of Sibol ng Laguna, demonstrating the technical skill, creativity, and competitive spirit of University of Cabuyao Computer Science students.

  5. Present day

    A community for aspiring computer scientists

    ACSS continues to promote academic excellence, camaraderie, leadership, and technical development while encouraging participation in campus activities and computing competitions.
