OPENING PRINCIPLES

Preamble

We, the Computer Science students at the University of Cabuyao, recognizing the vital necessity of a unified, proactive, and resilient student body, do hereby promulgate this Constitution.

Guided by the foundational principles of collaboration, integrity, and academic excellence, we commit ourselves to the holistic personal, intellectual, and professional development of every member. We strive to mold globally competitive, ethically grounded, and value-driven individuals equipped to address and solve real-world technological challenges.

Through inclusive programs, rigorous academic initiatives, and the cultivation of a vibrant community, we seek to establish a legacy of innovation and leadership that benefits the University, the nation, and society.

United by technology and learning

ACSS is grounded in academic excellence, service, integrity, teamwork, and the responsible use of technology.

Direction

Mission and Vision

M

Mission

The Association of Computer Science Students (ACSS) is dedicated to nurturing globally competent, innovative, and values-oriented computer science students. By fostering a culture of collaboration, leadership, and continuous learning, we empower our members with the skills, mindset, and character needed to excel in their fields and make meaningful contributions to the University, the community, and the nation.

V

Vision

To be a leading and inclusive academic organization that inspires computer science students to become innovative thinkers, ethical leaders, and lifelong learners. We envision a vibrant community grounded in excellence, creativity, and unity, driving forward technological advancement and national development.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Core Values

Three principles shape how ACSS members learn, work, and serve together.

C

Collaborative Innovation

We continuously develop our knowledge, technical abilities, and problem-solving skills to achieve excellence in Computer Science.

U

Unwavering Integrity

We use our skills and resources to support fellow students, the university, and the wider community.

C

Commitment to Excellence

We act with honesty, responsibility, fairness, and respect in every academic and organizational activity.

Objectives

Turning principles into action

  1. Enhance students’ knowledge and technical skills through workshops, seminars, competitions, and other academic activities.
  2. Promote teamwork, leadership, creativity, and responsible use of technology among Computer Science students.
  3. Represent the interests, ideas, and concerns of Computer Science students within the university.
  4. Build stronger relationships among students, educators, university organizations, and industry professionals.
